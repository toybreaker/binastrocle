title: 'Binocle - Palazzo Mezzogiorno'
description: "The Milan Chamber of Commerce occupied a building of the early 1960s attributed to Antonio Cassi Ramelli, directly connected with Piazza Affari and characterized by a three-storey stone base with a set back volume above, clad in azure ceramic tiles. Our competition entry reopened the ground floor to the city and provided the existing building with a new top and a second facade."
year: 2018
program: mixed-use

Rather than as an independent palazzo, we treated the building as a fragment of the city with its two blocks slightly out of alignment and oriented towards the street at a different angle. Taking inspiration from Milanese modernism and observing Torre Velasca, Torre Turati and Corso Magenta 19 by GPA Monti, we investigated the potentials of a building that gets wider as it grows taller.

A careful demolition of selected volumes frees the volume needed to extend the rear body upwards until it achieves the proportions of a tower. The new structure provides a roof addition, a continuous facade and an internal window frame at the stone base. Depending on the level it passes, it becomes cladding, cavity, balcony or winter garden.
