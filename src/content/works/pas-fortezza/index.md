description: "Design for the new permanent exhibition on the history of South Tyrol at the Forte di Fortezza, a nineteenth-century fortress on the Brenner route. A reversible and adaptable family of display elements is dimensioned on the rhythm of the existing casemates and works as a new system in dialogue with the building."

The restricted competition concerned a general concept for the whole exhibition route and the detailed design of one sample room of about seventy square metres. We nevertheless proposed to reorganize the outdoor area, reshaping the forecourt into a gentle porphyry slope that removes the internal ramp, makes the first casemate available for the exhibition and turns the approach into a passage from landscape to architecture.

Inside, a single family of elements carries display cases, graphics, lighting and media. Nothing is fixed: the system stands clear of walls and vaults, and every room can be reconfigured leaving the monument untouched.

The vertical elements are cut from a single panel, so that the off-cut becomes a second element. The word *PAS*, title of the exhibition, overcomes the idea of limits and borders to focus instead on *passages* and connections.

Ten practices were admitted to the competition phase out of 104 applications. Among them, our team was awarded with the third prize.
